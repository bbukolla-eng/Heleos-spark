# CSI Division 23 delivery structure and completion contract

The master scope is the verified CSI/CSC Division 23 hierarchy. Its baseline is
**MasterFormat 2016 Numbers & Titles, April 2016**. The earlier agency-guide
organization is superseded in active planning. Original historical evidence and
accepted software remain preserved; no speculative agency crosswalk is adopted.
The separate current-2026 catalogue reconciliation is visible and does not stop
known-baseline implementation.

## Record responsibilities

| Record | Responsibility | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `docs/operations/build-goal.md` | Stable complete Division 23 product outcome and Mac-first delivery | Does not permanently select a next duct/device task |
| `docs/plans/division-23-section-register.json` | Exact CSI identities, parent/child tree, source provenance and coverage | Catalogue titles do not supply a mechanical taxonomy or ready assignments |
| `docs/plans/division-23-task-contracts.json` | Four planned task frames and criterion bindings for each node | Actual source/case/interface/path checks must be frozen before readiness |
| Master index and section cards | Full CSI lineup and each section's finite delivery endpoints | Coverage order is not a universal serial dependency |
| Completion matrix | Readable projection of register coverage | Never a second independently edited status authority |
| Selected task ledger and receipts | Exact scope, commands, expected/actual results, candidate identities, independent acceptance and integration | Historical pending results do not override later terminal disposition |
| `CURRENT_STATUS.md` | One live primary task, ready independent actions and next executable step | No repeated unchanged status-only work |

This structure creates no scheduler, recurring hook or background execution.
The existing active-build status check resolves checkout authority. Structural
validation checks record consistency, not product readiness or acceptance.

## What complete means for one task

A task has one independently testable deliverable and a frozen contract naming
scope, exclusions, input identities, approved rules, independent expected cases,
tolerances, actual output dependencies, sole writer, exact code/test paths,
interfaces, commands, reviewer and receipt location.

It is **accepted** when every mandatory criterion passes on those identified
bytes, independent review accepts that scope and integration/evidence are
recorded. A source-definition packet, calculation kernel, connected workflow,
recognition qualification and complete section are different deliverables.
One can close without falsely closing or reopening the others.

| State | Meaning and finite exit |
| --- | --- |
| planned | Required coverage identified; not an executable assignment |
| definition_pending | Specific missing contract fields and the action to establish them are recorded |
| ready | Frozen contract and exact required accepted outputs are available |
| in_progress | One assigned writer produces the bounded deliverable |
| review | Candidate and declared checks exist; mandatory findings cite criteria |
| accepted | Required checks, independent review and integration evidence recorded |
| blocked_input | Exact missing input, affected scope and resolution action identified; independent work continues |
| deferred | Explicit later lane, never an implicit prerequisite |

A justified `not_required_by_contract` disposition concerns that contract only.
Service scope can deliver obligations rather than quantities. A project's
not-applicable finding does not complete product support.

## Review protocol with an endpoint

1. Freeze candidate manifest and mandatory criteria. Scope/correctness reviewers
   can inspect the same candidate concurrently.
2. Each blocker cites a criterion or shared boundary, expected versus actual
   result, evidence/reproducer and affected paths. Enhancements are separate tasks.
3. Correct the failure and check the affected regression surface. Broaden checks
   only for changed dependencies, new defects or a recorded new risk.
4. When those findings close, independently accept/integrate and advance; another
   general review round is not automatically required.
5. A repeated failure after two correction rounds triggers a bounded diagnosis
   of the oracle/input/implementation assumption, not permission to waive it.
6. Expected UNKNOWN can pass its own case; it cannot replace a required known
   quantity. New criteria require an explicit scope/contract version.

The owner decides missing estimating policy and final release. Routine engineering
acceptance within existing authority belongs to coordinating Codex; it does not
require a new owner decision for every case, section or regression.

## What complete means for each section

These eight dimensions are coverage obligations, not eight compulsory assignments
or review rounds per item. One accepted change may satisfy several dimensions for
its exact bound scope. Every section includes all required descendants.

| ID | Required section result | Pass evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SC01 / TAX | Versioned CSI scope and complete required types, variants, attributes, relationships, exclusions, services and child references | Mechanical source inventory reconciles with zero unexplained omissions; required taxonomy/relationship/child identities resolve |
| SC02 / EVID | Required drawing, schedule and specification evidence plus explicit ambiguity/omission behavior | Frozen original-source cases preserve source/revision/page/region or clause and meet declared recognition/extraction checks |
| SC03 / RULE | Applicable approved basis for every required quantity/obligation | Rule/version bindings and independent cases cover inclusion, exclusion, lifecycle, package and unknown treatment |
| SC04 / CALC | Deterministic quantities or supported nonnumeric obligations | All fixed cases match exact counts/states or predeclared measurement tolerances; procurement/installation boundaries remain correct |
| SC05 / RECON | Plans, schedules, specs, assemblies and responsibilities agree or produce expected exceptions | Duplicate, conflict, omission and package cases pass without invented quantities or double procurement |
| SC06 / EDIT | Corrections, source revisions, recalculation and reopening work in the application | Fixed lifecycle cases preserve history and invalidate affected results only |
| SC07 / OUT | Estimator Excel and evidence PDF agree with accepted current results | Expected rows/totals, unknown/stale presentation and original-source references pass; artifacts reopen successfully |
| SC08 / ACCEPT | Complete required section scope works on Mac for the frozen representative set | All mandatory taxonomy/children/dimensions have accepted evidence and the independently reviewed section dossier passes |

A parent's completion aggregates its own scope and every required child receipt
without rerunning unchanged child reviews. A shared counter/parser/export is
supporting evidence; it does not independently close the parent. Section scope
is bounded by identified sources/editions and declared required types; changed
editions or added support create versioned extensions.

Full product acceptance additionally requires current-edition catalogue
reconciliation, representative product acceptance and later Windows delivery.
Those later gates do not reopen accepted Mac engineering milestones.

## Execution and progress

The [master plan](../plans/2026-09-16-division23-section-delivery.md) exposes the
complete verified baseline tree; the [task contracts](2026-09-16-division23-section-task-contracts.md)
define every section's deliveries. Keep one primary implementation in root status.
Preserve the existing approved `EQUIPMENT-COUNT-1` priority as shared support;
other sections proceed when their own inputs are ready. Neither duct/air-device
recognition nor undefined unrelated sections are all-category gates.

Before knowledge-dependent work, follow the recorded CSI NotebookLM routing,
reuse applicable verified findings and verify consequential source passages.
Provide the hash-pinned packet to bounded Claude assignments when available;
Codex works on nonoverlapping paths and independently checks candidates.
Provider unavailability blocks that dispatch only.

At completion, record exact evidence/outcome and advance root status in the same
checkpoint. Reopen work only for changed relevant bytes, a concrete defect or a
recorded unresolved acceptance condition. Report actual delivered capability,
remaining mandatory gaps and the next executable action; a classification
migration or structural check is not mechanical takeoff implementation.
